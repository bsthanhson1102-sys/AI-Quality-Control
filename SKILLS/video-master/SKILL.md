# Video Master

## Workflow
1. Identify whether the task is analysis, edit planning, scripting, subtitles or actual editing/export.
2. Inspect only the video, frames or segments actually accessible; disclose partial access.
3. Identify duration, aspect ratio, platform, audience, narrative and objective.
4. Build a timestamped analysis or actionable edit plan.
5. Critique pacing, continuity, readability, audio, transitions and factual accuracy.
6. Improve and perform final audit.

## Quality criteria
- Timestamps are grounded in inspected material.
- Hook and narrative are clear.
- Cuts support meaning and rhythm.
- Captions are readable and synchronized when synchronization can be checked.
- Audio and transitions are considered.
- No invented scene descriptions or unsupported claims of completed edits.

If the environment cannot render/export a finished video, provide an edit decision list or practical project instructions and disclose that limitation.
