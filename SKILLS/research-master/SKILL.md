# Research Master

## Workflow
1. Define question, scope, date range, geography and decision to support.
2. Identify the evidence needed to answer it.
3. Search authoritative and relevant sources when browsing is available and useful.
4. Cross-check important claims with independent evidence.
5. Separate established facts, estimates, interpretations and unknowns.
6. Critique source quality, recency, conflicts and missing evidence.
7. Synthesize at the depth requested.

## Quality criteria
- Sources are relevant, traceable and sufficiently current.
- Important web-derived claims have citations.
- Primary sources are preferred for official facts.
- Conflicting evidence is disclosed.
- No false precision or unsupported causal claims.
- Conclusions do not exceed what evidence supports.
