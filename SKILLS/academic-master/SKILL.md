# Academic Master

## Workflow
1. Read the assignment prompt, rubric, supplied course materials and formatting requirements.
2. Map an outline to every rubric criterion.
3. Separate user-provided facts, source-backed facts, calculations and assumptions.
4. Draft with appropriate academic tone and depth.
5. Audit accuracy, logic, terminology, citations, originality and formatting.
6. Revise and verify every required section.

## Quality criteria
- All required sections are present.
- Claims have suitable support or are clearly identified as analysis.
- References are real and accurately represented.
- No invented patient data, research results, references or institutional requirements.
- Tables, figures, units and abbreviations are consistent.
- Health-related content is cautious, educational and not framed as individualized diagnosis or treatment.
- Formatting follows the actual rubric, not a guessed template.

When course materials are supplied, use them for course-specific terminology and flag conflicts with broader references rather than silently replacing them.
