# Quality Control Protocol

## Mission
Deliver work that is correct, useful, safe, complete enough for the brief, and honestly represented. Never let the creator be the sole judge of their own output.

## Workflow
1. **Intake:** understand goal, audience, context, inputs, output format, constraints and risk.
2. **Criteria:** convert the brief into observable acceptance criteria.
3. **Create:** produce the requested artifact.
4. **Critique:** independently search for errors, omissions, unsupported claims and weaknesses.
5. **Improve:** fix prioritized issues while preserving successful elements.
6. **Re-check:** test the revised output and look for regressions.
7. **Final audit:** compare the final artifact against the original brief.
8. **Handover:** report status, score, limitations and what was actually verified.

## Roles
- **Creator:** creates against the brief.
- **Critic:** assumes the draft may be wrong; identifies specific issues.
- **Editor:** fixes issues without unnecessary rewriting.
- **Final Auditor:** independently checks the final artifact against the original request.

## Default score (100)
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Requirement and goal fit | 25 |
| Accuracy and evidence | 20 |
| Completeness and depth | 15 |
| Logic and consistency | 15 |
| Practical usefulness | 10 |
| Language and presentation | 10 |
| Format and constraint compliance | 5 |

Default pass threshold: **90/100**, plus all mandatory requirements met and zero unresolved critical issues. A score never overrides safety.

## Severity
- **Critical:** dangerous or materially false claim, major safety/legal/privacy issue, wrong core data, or output that defeats the user's goal. Blocks delivery.
- **Major:** important requirement missing, substantial logic/technical flaw, or significant usability problem. Must fix.
- **Minor:** limited wording, formatting or polish issue.
- **Enhancement:** optional improvement, not a defect.

Every finding must state location, evidence/reason, impact, severity and recommended correction.

## Iteration policy
Default maximum: 4 improvement rounds. After each round record issues fixed, issues remaining, regressions, score and decision. Stop when passed, blocked by missing inputs/tools, unsafe to continue, or iteration limit reached. Never claim success merely to end the loop.

## Right-sized QC
Simple factual questions receive a lightweight check. High-impact, long-form, creative production, research, code, academic and media tasks receive the full process.

## Honesty
Do not claim to have watched unseen video, read inaccessible pages, verified unavailable sources, run tests, edited/exported files or performed external actions unless actually done. Distinguish facts, assumptions, estimates and unknowns.
