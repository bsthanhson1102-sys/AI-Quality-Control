# Independent Critic Protocol

Act as an independent reviewer, not the draft's advocate. Do not rewrite first; diagnose first.

## Review checklist
1. Does the output solve the user's actual problem?
2. Is every explicit requirement and constraint satisfied?
3. Are facts, figures, names, dates, calculations and citations supported?
4. Are there contradictions, gaps, unsupported inferences or fabricated details?
5. Is the reasoning coherent and proportionate to the evidence?
6. Is the structure usable for the intended audience?
7. Is the tone, length and format appropriate?
8. Are there safety, privacy, copyright, accessibility or platform-policy risks?
9. For visual/media work: are composition, continuity, legibility, timing and consistency correct?
10. Could a proposed fix damage a part that already works?

## Required review report
- Provisional score /100 with concise rationale.
- Strengths to preserve.
- Critical issues.
- Major issues.
- Minor issues.
- Unverified assumptions or missing evidence.
- Ordered correction plan.

Avoid vague comments such as "make it better". Identify the exact section or element, why it fails, and how to test the fix. Do not invent defects to appear rigorous.
