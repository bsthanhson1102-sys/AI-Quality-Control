## 4. Creator artifact

### Saturday — Declutter and sort (about 2 hours)

1. 15 minutes: prepare rubbish bags, cleaning cloths, sorting baskets and ventilation.
2. 25 minutes: clear shared living areas and return misplaced items.
3. 30 minutes: sort clothes into keep, wash, donate/give away and discard.
4. 30 minutes: tidy the kitchen, wipe surfaces, check expired food and organize utensils.
5. 20 minutes: take out rubbish, start laundry and put supplies away.

### Sunday — Clean and finish (about 90 minutes)

1. 20 minutes: dust surfaces and equipment.
2. 25 minutes: clean the bathroom and high-soil areas.
3. 25 minutes: vacuum or sweep, then mop floors.
4. 10 minutes: replace bedding or towels if needed.
5. 10 minutes: final walkthrough and return remaining items to their places.

Execution principles: work from high to low and dry to wet; do not empty every storage area at once; place undecided items in one temporary box; take short breaks when needed.

## 5. Critic findings

- **T08-F01 — Minor:** The initial plan did not explicitly warn against mixing household cleaning chemicals. This is a preventable safety omission. **Correction:** add a clear instruction not to mix bleach with acids, ammonia or other cleaning products, and follow product labels.
- **T08-F02 — Minor:** The plan could imply that every decluttering decision must be made immediately. This may slow the task and create unnecessary fatigue. **Correction:** retain a single temporary decision box and defer uncertain items.
- **T08-F03 — Enhancement:** Time estimates vary by home size. **Correction:** label the durations as approximate and allow unfinished low-priority tasks to move to a later session.

## 6. Editor changes and re-check

The final plan:
- keeps the two-day schedule and approximate time blocks;
- explicitly preserves a temporary box for undecided items;
- adds the safety rule: never mix bleach with acids, ammonia or other cleaning products; follow each product label and ventilate the area;
- states that time blocks are estimates, not a completion guarantee.

Re-check: all five acceptance criteria were reviewed against the revised plan. No specialist Skill was added, no unsupported external facts were needed, and the revisions did not remove any required task block.
